# Hi, I'm Resham 👋

I'm **Dr. Resham Raj Shivwanshi**. I teach and do research at the School of Computer Science, **UPES Dehradun**. Most of my working hours go into one question: can a computer look at a medical image, tell a doctor something useful, and be honest about when it might be wrong?

That question began with my PhD at **NIT Raipur**, where I spent five years building systems that find and assess lung nodules in CT scans. I still work on lung cancer imaging. I've also moved into photoacoustic imaging, which uses light and sound to see inside tissue, and into testing whether quantum computing really helps medical AI. Sometimes it does. Often it doesn't, and I think saying so plainly is part of the job.

Before UPES I taught at Woxsen University, worked on drones and computer vision at IIT Bhilai, and helped train large language models with human feedback at Turing. I started out in electronics and embedded systems, so I'm happiest when the code ends up running on something real: a Jetson board, a drone, or a robot in simulation.

I also run **NAIQ**, a small venture where research ideas get turned into tools people can actually use.

### What I work on

- 🫁 **Medical image analysis**: lung nodule detection, segmentation and radiomics
- 🔦 **Inverse problems** in photoacoustic and optical imaging
- ⚛️ **Quantum machine learning**, tested on real IBM quantum hardware
- 🚁 **Edge AI, UAVs and robotics simulation**
- 👁️ **Computer vision** with PyTorch, TensorFlow and OpenCV
